Mapped outlines are not a survey. Street lines show location, not curb width; they are flattened to one ground plane, with lower streets omitted. Heritage has a detailed facade reconstruction using city design drawings and published heights; its facade spacing and rooftop equipment remain approximate. Other roofs are simplified and some podium heights are estimated. This lets you inspect spacing; exact street widths and Chicago’s multiple road levels need additional data.
